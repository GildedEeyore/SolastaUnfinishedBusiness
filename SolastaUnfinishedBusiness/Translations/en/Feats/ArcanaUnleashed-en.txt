You gain the following benefits:\n• Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.\n• Additional Spells: When you have spell slots of a level listed below, you thereafter always have the spells listed for that level and lower prepared: 1st Chromatic Orb, 2nd Shatter, 3rd Fireball, 4th Vitriolic Sphere, 5th Cone of Cold (in place of Wall of Force, which Solasta does not implement).\n• Fueled Evocation: Once per turn when you cast an Evocation spell and deal damage, you can roll up to two of your unexpended Hit Dice and add the total rolled to one of the spell's damage rolls.
